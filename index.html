        Glossary

        F-1
        The nonimmigrant student classification for academic study in the United States.
        I-20
        "Certificate of Eligibility for Nonimmigrant Student Status," issued by your school. Your program end date, CPT authorizations, and OPT recommendation all appear on it.
        I-94
        The admission record showing your class of admission and how long you may stay. Under the Duration of Status final rule (effective mid-September 2026) it carries a fixed expiration date instead of the open-ended "D/S" notation. Retrieve yours at i94.cbp.dhs.gov.
        D/S (Duration of Status)
        The former open-ended admission period for F-1 students. The final rule replaces it with a fixed admission period, so your authorized stay can end before your program or OPT does.
        DSO
        Designated School Official — the person at your school authorized to update SEVIS, issue I-20s, and recommend CPT/OPT. Your single source of truth.
        SEVIS
        Student and Exchange Visitor Information System, the federal database that holds your F-1 record.
        CPT
        Curricular Practical Training — employment that is an integral part of your curriculum, authorized by your DSO on your I-20 before you start work. Twelve months or more of full-time CPT eliminates OPT eligibility at that degree level.
        OPT
        Optional Practical Training — up to 12 months of work authorization per higher degree level, most often used after graduation ("post-completion OPT").
        STEM-OPT extension
        A 24-month extension of post-completion OPT for students with a qualifying STEM degree working for an E-Verify employer under a formal training plan.
        EAD
        Employment Authorization Document — the physical card from USCIS. Its "valid from" and "valid to" dates define your actual work period, not the dates you requested.
        Form I-765
        Application for Employment Authorization — what you file with USCIS for OPT and STEM-OPT.
        Form I-983
        The training plan you and your employer complete for STEM-OPT, including self-evaluations at 12 months and at the end of the extension.
        Form I-539
        Application to Extend/Change Nonimmigrant Status. With fixed I-94 end dates, you may need this to extend your authorized stay if your I-94 expires before a filing window closes.
        Grace period
        Time to depart, transfer, or change status after your authorized activity ends: 60 days after program completion, 30 days after post-completion OPT ends, 60 days after STEM-OPT ends.
        Cap-gap
        An automatic extension of F-1 status, and sometimes work authorization, for students with a timely-filed cap-subject H-1B petition requesting an October 1 start date.
        Unemployment days
        Aggregate days without qualifying employment during OPT: 90 days on post-completion OPT, plus 60 more (150 total) during a STEM-OPT extension.

        Before you rely on any date in this app

        1. Pull your current I-94 and confirm the exact expiration date.
        2. Compare your I-20 program end date against what your DSO has in SEVIS.
        3. Send your DSO the dates this app produced and ask them to confirm.

        Where your data lives

        This app has no backend, no database, and no analytics. It is a folder of static files. Every date you type is calculated and stored by JavaScript running in your own browser, in localStorage under this site's origin. Whoever published this app cannot read it — there is nowhere for it to be sent.

        • Calendar export builds the .ics file in memory and hands it to your browser's download. Your calendar app does the reminding from then on, offline.
        • Google Drive backup is optional. Your browser talks directly to Google with your own login and the narrow drive.file scope, which only grants access to files this app itself created.
